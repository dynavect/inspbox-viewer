Drag with the left mouse button to rotate. Scroll to zoom. Use the middle mouse button or right-drag to pan.

Use the component tree to select parts and toggle their eye icons. Right-click a part for isolation and fit options.

Open enclosure hides the large cover. Show all restores every exported part. Faceplate zooms in on the controls and lettering.

The ruler tool measures between selected points or parallel faces. Enclosure measurements are shown in millimetres. This browser model is a visual mesh; use the manufacturing CAD for precision work. Reset explode and section before measuring.

Explode is an illustrative separation, not an assembly sequence. Section cuts are open and do not add solid cut faces.

The PCB, attached display and board connectors are absent from this file. Other enclosure components and lettering are included.
